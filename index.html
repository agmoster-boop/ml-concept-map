NODE_03 // REINFORCEMENT

TARGET: REWARD_OPTIMIZATION

> DEPLOYED_USE_CASE
Autonomous Robotics
> ALGORITHM_MODULES

Mechanism: A model-free algorithm that builds a lookup table (Q-table) to evaluate the expected utility of taking specific actions in given environmental states.

Strengths: Does not require a pre-built mathematical model of the environment and guarantees convergence to optimal policies in finite state spaces.

Use Cases: Strategic game-playing AI, dynamic traffic signal management, and grid navigation.

Mechanism: Combines Q-Learning with Deep Neural Networks to approximate Q-values rather than using a lookup table.

Strengths: Capable of handling massive, continuous, or high-dimensional state spaces where lookup tables become computationally impossible.

Use Cases: Autonomous vehicle control, robotic manipulation, and complex video game strategy.
